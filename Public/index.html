Introduction

A welfare-based educational project in South Punjab

Al-Azan Educational Complex is a grand welfare based educational project located in South Punjab, approximately two kilometers from the city of Alipur on Jatoi Road. A total area of twenty kanals has been allocated for this project, where construction is progressing in phases according to a comprehensive and well organized master plan.

By the grace and blessings of Almighty Allah and through the beneficence of His Beloved Messenger, the first phase was completed within a remarkably short period of one year and three months. During this phase, the first building named “Umar bin Abdul Aziz (RA) Block” was constructed over an area of 15,571 square feet and is now fully operational for educational activities.

Alongside this, construction of “Jamia Masjid Al-Suffah” commenced over an area of 10,600 square feet. The grey structure of the mosque has been completed, while finishing and some essential works are still in progress.

Foundational Objectives

Vision, Mission & Slogan

Objectives

  1. Revival and dissemination of the sciences of the Quran and Hadith.
  2. Rectification of beliefs and understanding of fundamental jurisprudential rulings in light of authentic Islamic teachings and moral purification (tazkiyah).
  3. Promotion of the Islamic concept of moderation (wasatiyyah).
  4. Equipping individuals with essential life skills for personal and societal development.
  5. Preparing distinguished scholars and leaders capable of addressing future intellectual, academic and social challenges.

Vision

To contribute towards establishment of an exemplary society by nurturing the younger generation with balanced integration of religious and contemporary education.

Slogan

Character building institution with religious and contemporary education

Mission

  1. Promotion and dissemination of religious sciences aligned with modern needs and advancements.
  2. Cultivating capable, ethical and well-rounded individuals through high-quality education and comprehensive training.
  3. Addressing social issues, intellectual confusion, moral decline and rising intolerance through educational and ethical initiatives.
  4. Strengthening societal morals by promoting Islamic ethical values and principles.
  5. Empowering underprivileged communities toward self-sufficiency and sustainable development.

Future Development Plan 2026

Goals for the Year 2026

  1. Establishment of a modern Computer Lab for basic Computer Science and Information Technology education.
  2. Installation of CCTV cameras for effective security monitoring and campus safety.
  3. Development of a Digital Language Lab for specialized Spoken English and Arabic training.
  4. Establishment of a well-equipped library to promote research, reading and academic excellence.
  5. Provision of air-conditioned classrooms for a comfortable learning environment.
  6. Development of an online portal enabling parents to monitor students’ academic performance and progress.
